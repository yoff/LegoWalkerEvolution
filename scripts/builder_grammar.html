Three languages, one construction.

1 / Walking program

Bodies, hinges, transmissions, motor clock and logical feet describe the motion to preserve.

2 / Physical productions

Stock parts, legal socket stacks, drive planes and bounded brackets realize that program.

3 / Proof obligations

Every part and connection needs provenance. New supports create new frame-path obligations.

4 / External tests

Exact reconstruction, collision checks and fresh dynamics decide whether the construction is usable.

Why the initial pose is not enough

The support must miss the whole sweep.

Illustrative four-bar geometry, not the qualified robot's solved linkage. The dashed crank envelope is an advisory keepout; it does not check every moving part.

Keep motion checks separate from drawings.

The builder ranks supports against sampled reference-clock motion and conservative reservations, including shaft envelopes and the motor's side bracket.

Exact checks still decide acceptance. A zero-time fit, a clear sampled pose, or a complete derivation cannot prove between-sample collision safety.

The dynamic gate then tests a free chassis with self-collision and designated ground-contact regions. It rejects unsafe prefixes without inventing partial fitness.

What the search learns

Reported parts and bodies identify producing choices and dependency paths. Cheap stock/layout alternatives are tried before broader link-layer repairs.

Only a scoped, exact failed witness becomes a hard nogood. An advisory hint can prioritize alternatives, not ban an entire beam or shaft family.

Cost-ranked, bounded depth-first continuation; frozen checkpoints and evidence hashes. Exhausting states, frontier or checks is not proof of impossibility.

Where autonomy currently ends

  • Automatic: exact collision/connectivity feedback → bounded construction backtracking.
  • Automatic: safe, consistently backward gait → one motor-direction child → fresh trial. {{FEEDBACK_SOURCE}}
  • Enumerated mutation: physical gear reduction is available when the current geometry supports it.
  • Not wired yet: slow/stalled torque-limited movement → automatic reduction request → packaging-proof search.
  • Externally directed: new design goals, target tolerances, grammar extensions and policy promotion. The builder selects legal alternatives inside those boundaries.

The grammar is a bounded constructive theory, not a general LEGO CAD solver. It does not certify strength, axial retention, thermal endurance, assembly order or arbitrary chassis designs.
