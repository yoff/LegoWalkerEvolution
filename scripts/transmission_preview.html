Construct transmission from walking demands

Offline transmission subassemblies, not complete robots. Every gear and axle is constructed afresh. Walking motion is checked against the original clock, even when the motor must run in the opposite direction. New bearings, chassis and mounting parts are still required; collisions and dynamics are not qualified.
